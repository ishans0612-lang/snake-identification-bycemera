Snake Safety Guide

Stay safe

  • Keep away from snakes.
  • Never try to catch, touch, or kill a snake.
  • Give wildlife room to move away.

Suspected snakebite

  • Seek emergency medical care immediately.
  • Keep the person calm and limit unnecessary movement.
  • Do not use home remedies or delay care to identify the snake.

Important

This app cannot replace medical professionals. A photograph cannot establish that a snake is safe.

About this project

This is an educational wildlife app. Its initial sample catalog is limited and has not been fully validated for detailed field identification. Image recognition is not configured.

Species photos are searched from Wikimedia Commons by scientific name. Each displayed image links to its Commons file page and shows available license attribution.
